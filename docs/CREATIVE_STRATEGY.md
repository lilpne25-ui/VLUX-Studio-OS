# Creative Strategy

VLUX content starts with the operational reality of a business:

```text
business problem
→ economic or operational consequence
→ VLUX solution
→ business benefit
→ call to action
```

The Creative Director avoids generic motion and feature-first tutorials. It proposes industrial situations such as CAD/ERP handoffs, quality traceability, product engineering reuse, manufacturing data, and automation.

The output is a reviewable brief with hooks, narrative structure, scene intent, 3D direction, risks, CTA, and metrics to observe later. It does not claim customer outcomes or generate a final video.
