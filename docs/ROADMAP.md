# Roadmap

## Delivered

- V0.1 — dataset foundation.
- V0.2 — Creative Intelligence and commercial schema.
- V0.3 — local dashboard and end-to-end workflow.
- V0.4 — platform-separated analytics, EP011 activation and EP025 proposal comparison.
- V0.5 — Attention Lab, layered A/B, human evaluation ledger, Dataset Registry, Training Gate, Model Registry, local production versions and rollback.
- V0.6 — Creative Feedback Loop: versioned contracts, actionable recommendations, production handoff, episode-version import, version comparison, human evaluation, performance records and loop dashboard/CLI.

## Next, in order

1. **Human evidence** — record identified 1–5 evaluations for real episodes; keep anonymous and AI-assisted inputs excluded from the gate.
2. **Commercial evidence** — attach verified leads, meetings, proposals or sales with date and provenance; never derive them from likes.
3. **Controlled experiment** — run the EP021 opening change (visual change before 2 s) as one variable, with 3-second retention as the primary metric, when real retention data exists.
4. **Authorized dataset expansion** — add independent episodes until the Training Gate can pass.
5. **Evaluation** — compare the rules baseline with a candidate under complete-episode holdout.
6. **Manual promotion** — require a named human approver and retain rollback.
7. **Integration** — consume recommendations and production contracts from VLUX Video OS through versioned interfaces.

## Not planned as autonomous behavior

- automatic publishing;
- automatic model promotion;
- automatic replacement of creative judgment;
- training on unauthorized, synthetic-only or pilot-only data;
- claims of virality prediction or customer impact without evidence.

Planned work is not presented as implemented capability.
